Privacy Policy: Truth Thread

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

Truth Thread (package: com.iqbuild.khayt_alhaqiqa) is a mobile detective game provided by iq games.

Privacy questions and deletion requests: iqgames300@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

DataWhy
A random device identifier generated by the game (not linked to your name, email or phone number)Save your progress and purchases, run competitions, prevent cheating
The text of the questions you type to suspects during interrogationsSent to our server to generate the suspect's reply
Advertising ID, device and usage data collected by the ad networkShow and measure rewarded ads
Purchase information (processed by Google Play)Deliver gems and VIP subscriptions. We never see your card details

We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not access your camera or microphone.

3. How we use it

  • Run the game, save progress and restore purchases.
  • Generate interrogation replies.
  • Show rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
  • Prevent cheating, fix bugs and improve the game.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers we need to operate the game:

  • Ad network (Unity LevelPlay and its partner networks): receives the advertising ID and device data to serve ads. They have their own privacy policies.
  • Google Play: processes purchases and subscriptions.
  • Hosting and server (Supabase): stores progress, runs competitions, and counts the number of questions per device per day. We do not store the text of your questions on our server.
  • AI provider (Anthropic): receives the text of your question, the evidence you have collected and the conversation so far, through our server, to generate the suspect's reply. We do not send your name or email; the random device identifier stays on our server and is not passed on.
  • Voice provider (ElevenLabs): receives the text of the suspect's reply (not your question) to turn it into speech.

We do not sell your data.

5. Security and retention

Data travels between the game and our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We keep data as long as it is needed to run the game and delete it when you ask.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: watching ads is always optional. You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Deletion: email iqgames300@gmail.com with a description of your device and the approximate dates you played, and we will delete the data linked to it. Uninstalling the game only removes local data.
  • Access and correction: you may ask for a copy of what we hold about you or for it to be corrected.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. AI-generated content

Suspect replies and voices in interrogations are generated automatically and may contain mistakes. Do not type real personal information into interrogation questions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page. We change the "Last updated" date at the top each time, and notify you in the game for material changes.
